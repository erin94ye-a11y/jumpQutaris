What is recorded

Information collected

Your IP address, approximate IP location and network provider; browser, operating system, device type, languages, time zone, screen dimensions, and available hardware and connection information.

How it’s used

To compare network location, language and time zone. These details do not establish your nationality or prove where you are physically located.

Records are accessible to the administrator and retained for 30 days. When network classification is configured, your IP is sent to ipapi.is for approximate location and network classification; otherwise it is sent to ipwho.is for approximate location only. No precise GPS location is requested.

Network checks can be incomplete or incorrect. They cannot prove that an IP is residential or that no VPN is in use.
