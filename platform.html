How metrics are measured

Virtual return

The percentage change in virtual equity from the initial virtual balance, including open positions.

Measured accuracy

Profitable completed virtual trades divided by all completed virtual trades. This measures the session's winning trade share, not AI predictive accuracy. It is unavailable until a trade closes.

Maximum virtual drawdown

The largest observed decline in virtual equity from its session peak.

AI confidence

A decision-level estimate from Qutara. It is separate from measured trading accuracy.
